ClimaSense Help Center

Frequently asked questions, meteorological definitions, and troubleshooting assistance.

How does ClimaSense determine the Comfort Score?
The Comfort Score (0-100) is synthesized from ambient temperature (calibrated to the 18°C-26°C human thermal neutrality band), relative humidity (optimal between 35%-65%), wind velocity, and local air quality index. Scores of 85+ reflect optimal outdoor recreation conditions.
Where does the scenic background photography originate?
Dynamic city scenery is fetched exclusively via Unsplash's verified photographic catalog. When searches are performed, our backend matches the city alongside the current atmospheric condition, respecting attribution guidelines and caching responses for rapid loading.
How are the 4,000+ word City Encyclopedia articles compiled?
Articles are generated through a 38-section municipal monograph architecture that pulls authentic geographical coordinates, census demographics, historical milestones, and climate records from authoritative open repositories, computing verified word counts directly on the backend.
What happens if an API key expires or is throttled?
ClimaSense features multi-tiered fallback engineering. If OpenWeather or Gemini rate limits are reached, the system smoothly transitions to cached municipal records and grounded deterministic rules without crashing or displaying raw stack traces.
