Architecture & Design Standards

ClimaSense is engineered with strict production standards for data integrity, privacy, visual beauty, and responsiveness:

  • Dedicated 3D Atmospheric Simulation: Realistic layered cloud drift, precipitation particles, and astronomical cycles contained safely within a dedicated light-bounded stage.
  • Comprehensive 38-Section Municipal Monograph: Asynchronously loaded city discovery article exceeding 4,000 verified words with live word count calculation.
  • Zero-Leak API Key Security: All upstream keys (Gemini, OpenWeather, Unsplash) remain strictly protected on the backend.
  • Permanent Pure Light UI: Warm sunshine, soft mint green, and pale lavender accents over white surfaces with zero dark themes.
