ClimaSense Privacy Commitment

Last updated: October 2026. ClimaSense is committed to responsible, transparent, and privacy-respecting meteorological intelligence.

1. Information We Collect

We collect search queries, voluntary user accounts (username and hashed passwords), and optional browser geolocation coordinates requested solely to retrieve local weather data. We never sell, rent, or monetize your location history.

2. Upstream Integrations & Data Security

Weather data queries sent to OpenWeather and image queries sent to Unsplash are strictly anonymized on the backend. No personal identity tokens, cookies, or user credentials are transmitted to third-party photography or meteorological providers.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

Session cookies are used solely for authenticated user persistence. Client-side local storage records temperature and wind unit preferences without tracking across external domains.
